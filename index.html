The Language of Generative and Agentic AI

Core vocabulary — tokens, context windows, agents, RAG, evals, MCP, and everything in between.

📚
Generative AI for Everyone (DeepLearning.AI)Course
Andrew Ng's best non-technical intro to generative AI. The cleanest starting point for anyone new to the field — explains what AI can and can't do without requiring technical background.
🗄
Generative AI Genius Free Course (LevelUp Labs)Repo
Applied free course on building generative AI applications end to end. Complements the handbook with hands-on modules covering the full GenAI development cycle.
📄
On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models (Stanford, 2021)Paper
The paper that coined "foundation model" in 2021. The first 20 pages give the clearest definition in the field — essential reading for understanding what GPT, Claude, and Gemini actually are.
📝
Mapping the Mind of a Large Language Model (Anthropic)Blog
Most accessible serious read on what's actually happening inside an LLM. Builds the intuition needed to understand why models behave both magically and wrongly.
📝
What Is ChatGPT Doing and Why Does It Work? (Wolfram)Blog
The most useful long-form explainer on LLM mechanics by Stephen Wolfram. Explains probabilistic text prediction with unmatched clarity — a classic reference.
🔧
OpenAI Tokenizer ToolTool
Interactive tool showing how text gets split into tokens. Five minutes here gives deep intuition about token costs and why prompt wording affects both quality and price.
📄
Lost in the Middle: How LLMs Use Long Contexts (2023)Paper
Research showing attention quality degrades for content in the middle of long prompts. Critical for understanding why large context windows don't automatically produce better results.
📖
Anthropic: Long Context Prompting TipsDocs
Applied guide for getting more out of large context windows. Practical tricks for document processing and retrieval design in production systems.
📄
Training Language Models to Follow Instructions (InstructGPT/RLHF)Paper
OpenAI's foundational paper on instruction tuning and RLHF — the technique that turned raw language models into assistants. Explains what post-training is and why it matters.
🗄
Fine-Tuning 101 (LevelUp Labs)Repo
Applied guide on when fine-tuning beats prompting and when it doesn't. Helps you decide if your use case actually warrants the cost and complexity of fine-tuning.
📄
Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in LLMs (Wei et al., 2022)Paper
The Google Brain paper showing "think step by step" dramatically improves accuracy on math and logic. Foundation of every reasoning technique that followed — worth reading in full.
📝
Learning to Reason with LLMs (OpenAI)Blog
Explains OpenAI's o-series reasoning models and how test-time compute improves accuracy on hard problems. Essential for understanding when to use reasoning vs. standard models.
📖
Anthropic: Extended Thinking DocumentationDocs
How to use Claude's extended thinking mode for complex reasoning tasks. Shows practical tradeoffs between speed, cost, and accuracy for agentic workflows.
📄
DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMsPaper
Details DeepSeek's reasoning model training approach. Important for understanding how reasoning models differ from standard LLMs and the diversity of approaches in the field.
🗄
Introduction to Multimodal LLMs (LevelUp Labs)Repo
How models handle images, audio, and video. Essential as enterprise AI moves beyond text-only workflows — covers practical multimodal application patterns.
📄
Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal ModelsPaper
Google's primary technical report on Gemini's multimodal capabilities. Useful for understanding state-of-the-art multimodal performance and architecture choices.
📖
Anthropic: Vision DocumentationDocs
How to use Claude with images, PDFs, and documents. Practical guide for building document-processing pipelines that replace traditional OCR.
📖
Anthropic: Prompt Engineering OverviewDocs
The primary provider-authored reference for prompting Claude. Read this cover to cover — it's the clearest applied guide to the craft.
📖
OpenAI: Prompt Engineering GuideDocs
OpenAI's equivalent prompting reference. Read both provider guides to understand universal principles and model-specific differences.
🗄
Applied LLMs Mastery — Week 2: Prompting (LevelUp)Repo
Structured course module covering the full taxonomy of prompting techniques. Good complement to the individual provider guides with worked examples.
📖
Anthropic: System Prompts GuideDocs
How to write effective system prompts — role definition, rules, scope, and sticky instructions. The building block of every production AI system.
📄
Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3 Paper)Paper
The paper that introduced "few-shot learning" as a concept. Shows how in-context examples dramatically change model behavior without any fine-tuning.
📖
Anthropic: Multi-Shot PromptingDocs
The practical how-to for few-shot prompting — the single highest-leverage prompting technique. Two to three good examples often outperform a carefully engineered zero-shot prompt.
📝
Effective Context Engineering for AI Agents (Anthropic)Blog
The clearest public writeup on managing what goes into the context window for production agents. Covers ordering, compression, retrieval timing, and tool schema design.
📖
Anthropic: Tool Use DocumentationDocs
How to implement tool use with Claude — schema design, tool descriptions, error handling, and response parsing. The primary reference for building tool-using agents.
📖
OpenAI: Function Calling GuideDocs
OpenAI's equivalent tool use guide. Shows the function-calling pattern that became standard across providers — worth reading alongside Anthropic's guide.
📝
Building Effective Agents (Anthropic) ★ MUST READBlog
The most cited essay on agent design. Introduces the workflow vs. agent distinction, the five core workflow patterns, and when agentic complexity is actually worth adding. Read it twice.
🗄
Agents 101 Guide (LevelUp Labs)Repo
Structured walkthrough of agent concepts for practitioners. Good supplement to Anthropic's essay with more detailed breakdowns of agent components.
📝
How We Built Our Multi-Agent Research System (Anthropic)Blog
Honest writeup on the tradeoffs of multi-agent systems. Covers failure modes, debugging complexity, and real lessons from building a production multi-agent system.
📄
Towards an AI Co-Scientist (Google DeepMind)Paper
A serious production multi-agent system from Google. Good example of what multi-agent systems actually look like at scale in a research setting.
🗄

Fast path into RAG concepts — chunking, indexing, retrieval, reranking. The best quick reference for understanding the full RAG pipeline.
📖
OpenAI: Embeddings GuideDocs
Cleanest applied reference for using embeddings. Covers cosine similarity, embedding models, use cases, and practical implementation patterns.
📄
Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-NetworksPaper
Research origins of semantic embeddings. Shows how BERT-based models create meaningful vector representations of sentences — the foundation of modern RAG retrieval.
🗄
pgvector (Postgres vector extension)Repo
Postgres extension for vector storage and similarity search. Clean docs that explain core vector database concepts even if you use a different database.
📖
Anthropic: Memory Tool DocumentationDocs
Anthropic's recommended pattern for persistent memory in AI systems. Shows how to structure memory storage, retrieval, and write policies.
📖
LangChain: Memory ConceptsDocs
Framework-level documentation on short and long-term memory patterns. Good for understanding the engineering mechanics before implementing custom memory.
📄
Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot ArenaPaper
The foundational research on using LLMs to evaluate other LLMs. Shows when LLM judges agree with humans and when they systematically disagree — required for building evals.
📝
Why Language Models Hallucinate (OpenAI)Blog
Solid primer on the mechanics of hallucination. Important for setting realistic expectations and understanding why retrieval, guardrails, and evals are the mitigation path.
📖
Google Vertex AI: Grounding DocumentationDocs
How Google implements grounding (tying answers to verified sources) in Vertex AI. Applied grounding patterns for enterprise systems that need traceable outputs.
📖
Anthropic: Citations DocumentationDocs
How Claude's citation feature works — building systems that attribute claims to specific document sections. Key for enterprise systems requiring source-backed answers.
📖
NVIDIA NeMo GuardrailsDocs
The leading open-source guardrail framework. Covers both input and output guardrails with configurable policies — good reference for building enterprise safety layers.
📝
Meta: Llama Guard — LLM-Based Input/Output SafeguardBlog
Meta's safety classifier for filtering AI inputs and outputs. Production-ready model for content safety — can be deployed as a standalone guardrail layer.
🔧
Microsoft Presidio — PII Detection & AnonymizationTool
Open-source PII detection and anonymization. Essential for enterprise systems handling sensitive customer data — detects and redacts names, emails, credit cards, etc. in prompts.
🔧
Arize Phoenix — AI Observability PlatformTool
Leading observability platform for AI systems. Free to start, captures full traces, enables prompt debugging, and feeds production data into eval pipelines.
🔧
LangSmith — Observability & EvaluationTool
LangChain's observability and evaluation platform. Strong for teams using LangChain/LangGraph — integrates traces directly with eval workflows.
📖
OpenTelemetry: GenAI Semantic ConventionsDocs
Open standard for AI observability. Standardizes what gets logged (prompts, tokens, latency) across providers and frameworks — important for vendor-neutral tracing setups.
📖
Model Context Protocol (MCP) — Official SpecificationDocs
The full technical spec for MCP — the open protocol for connecting AI models to tools, data sources, and systems. Fast becoming the "USB-C of AI integrations."
📝
Introducing the Model Context Protocol (Anthropic)Blog
Best short introduction to MCP. Explains why the protocol exists, what problem it solves, and why enterprise AI integration is heading toward this standard.
📖
OpenAI Agents SDK DocumentationDocs
OpenAI's official framework for building agent applications. Handles the orchestration plumbing: agent loop, tool calls, handoffs, and built-in tracing.
📖
LangGraph DocumentationDocs
LangChain's framework for stateful agent workflows. The most mature reference for production-grade agent orchestration with cycle support and persistence.
📚
AI Evals for Everyone (LevelUp Labs) ★ COMPANION COURSECourse

🗄
Awesome Generative AI Guide (LevelUp Labs) ★Repo
The most active curated AI resource on GitHub. Contains monthly paper roundups, free courses, interview prep, and learning roadmaps. Free and regularly updated — bookmark this.

📄 InterviewReady Research Paper Curriculum

Papers from the InterviewReady/ai-engineering-resources repo — organized by topic for the AI Engineering course.

📄
Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani et al., 2017)Core Architecture
The foundational transformer paper — every LLM today is built on this architecture. Understanding self-attention and multi-head attention is non-negotiable for AI engineering interviews.
📄
BERT: Pre-training Deep Bidirectional Transformers (Google, 2018)Vectorization
Foundation of modern embedding models. Understanding BERT's bidirectional pretraining explains why embedding models produce semantically meaningful vectors — critical for RAG architecture.
📄
FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Attention (2022)Optimization
The optimization that made long-context models practical. Understanding FlashAttention explains how frontier models can handle 200K+ token contexts without running out of memory.
📄
The Era of 1-bit LLMs: All LLMs in 1.58 Bits (2024)Optimization
Quantization pushed to the extreme — shows how 1-bit weights can match full-precision models. Understanding quantization is key for open-source model deployment and cost optimization.
📄
DSPy: Compiling Declarative Language Model Calls (2023)Context Engineering
Framework for automatically optimizing LLM prompts. Understanding DSPy shows the state of the art in programmatic context engineering and prompt optimization.
📄
Automated Unit Test Improvement using LLMs at MetaCase Study
Real-world case study of LLM deployment at scale at Meta. Shows the engineering decisions, eval framework, and production challenges of a major AI deployment.
📄
Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective SSMsSSM Architecture
Alternative to transformer architecture for sequence modeling. Understanding Mamba and SSMs is important for knowing the architectural landscape beyond transformers.
📄
Speculative Decoding (2022)Optimization
Technique for accelerating LLM inference using a smaller draft model. Understanding speculative decoding is essential for production latency optimization and understanding inference infrastructure.

🧱 Intermediate · Knowledge systems depth

Ingestion, metadata and permissions, ANN search internals, and serving retrieval behind an API.

🔧
Unstructured DocumentationTool
Parsing PDFs, slides and HTML into clean elements before chunking. Most RAG quality problems start at ingestion.
🗄
Docling (document conversion)Repo
Open-source converter for PDFs and Office files into structured text with tables and layout kept.
📝
Contextual Retrieval (Anthropic)Blog
Adds context to each chunk before embedding. A concrete ingestion-time fix for lost context.
📖
Pinecone: Filter by MetadataDocs
Pre-filtering vectors by tenant, ACL, or date. The basis of permissions-aware retrieval.
📄
HNSW: Efficient and Robust Approximate Nearest Neighbor SearchPaper
The graph index behind most vector databases. Explains the recall, latency and memory tradeoffs of vector search.
📖
FastAPI TutorialDocs
Request models, dependency injection and async endpoints for wrapping an LLM pipeline as a service.
📖
MDN: Server-Sent EventsDocs
The transport most chat UIs use to stream tokens from a FastAPI endpoint.

🧭 Advanced · Engineering track

What you can reach by building well: agent patterns, memory, GraphRAG, red teaming, routing, serving and caching. Needs systems skill, not model math.

📄
ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language ModelsPaper
The reason-act-observe loop that most agent frameworks implement.
📄
Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement LearningPaper
Agents that critique and retry using written feedback. A planning and self-correction pattern.
📄
MemGPT: Towards LLMs as Operating SystemsPaper
Paging memory in and out of context. Foundation for state, memory and compaction design.
📄
From Local to Global: A Graph RAG ApproachPaper
The GraphRAG paper: entity graphs and community summaries for questions over whole corpora.
🗄
Microsoft GraphRAGRepo
Reference implementation of the GraphRAG pipeline.
📖
OWASP Top 10 for LLM ApplicationsDocs
The standard threat list: prompt injection, insecure output handling, excessive agency and more.
📖
promptfoo: Red TeamingDocs
Automated adversarial test generation and scoring for LLM apps.
🗄
NVIDIA garak (LLM vulnerability scanner)Repo
Probe-based scanner for jailbreaks, leakage and toxicity.
📄
RouteLLM: Learning to Route LLMs with Preference DataPaper
Send easy queries to cheap models and hard ones to strong models. Quantifies the cost saving.
📖
LiteLLM DocumentationDocs
One interface over many providers, with routing, fallbacks and budgets.
📖
vLLM DocumentationDocs
The standard open-source inference server: continuous batching, prefix caching, OpenAI-compatible API.
📄
Efficient Memory Management for LLM Serving with PagedAttentionPaper
The vLLM paper. Explains the KV cache and why paging it raises throughput.
📖
Anthropic: Prompt CachingDocs
Provider-side caching of a stable prompt prefix. Cuts cost and latency on long prompts.
🗄
GPTCache (semantic cache)Repo
Return a stored answer for a semantically similar query.

🧮 Advanced · Deep learning and math track

Needs linear algebra, optimization and training know-how: fine-tuning, alignment, quantization, MoE and GPU kernels.

📄
LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language ModelsPaper
Train small adapter matrices instead of all weights. The basis of cheap fine-tuning.
📄
QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMsPaper
LoRA on a 4-bit base model. Fine-tune large models on one GPU.
📖

Library for LoRA and other parameter-efficient methods.
📄
Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)Paper
Preference alignment without a separate reward model or RL loop.
📖
Hugging Face TRLDocs
Training library for SFT, DPO and RLHF-style methods.
📄
GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training QuantizationPaper
Weight-only quantization to 3 or 4 bits with small quality loss.
📄
AWQ: Activation-aware Weight QuantizationPaper
Protects the salient weights when quantizing. Common choice for 4-bit serving.
📖
Hugging Face: Quantization OverviewDocs
Side-by-side of bitsandbytes, GPTQ, AWQ and others.
📄

Sparse expert routing. Origin of modern mixture-of-experts scaling.
📄
Mixtral of ExpertsPaper
A practical open MoE model: 8 experts, 2 active per token.
📝
Mixture of Experts Explained (Hugging Face)Blog
Readable walkthrough of experts, routers and serving tradeoffs.
📖
NVIDIA CUDA C++ Programming GuideDocs
Threads, blocks, memory hierarchy. The base for reading any kernel.
📖
Triton Language DocumentationDocs
Python DSL for writing GPU kernels without raw CUDA.
🗄
GPU MODE LecturesRepo
Community lecture series on CUDA, Triton and kernel optimization.
